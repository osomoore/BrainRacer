# BrainRacer — Status (as of 2026-11-22)

**Sixteen maps are playable:** City, Factory, Beach & Harbor, Mine, Farm, Sky City, Dark Forest, Underwater, and the eight worlds below.

Next work is planned in **[docs/NEXT.md](NEXT.md)**. The original design is in [docs/PLAN.md](PLAN.md), which this file supersedes where they differ.

Each world adds five cars and one companion to the shared collection. Brickworks adds Puff Cub and Frozen Tundra adds Square Snowman. Complete Underwater to unlock Brickworks. Completing three destination objectives in a race unlocks the next world in the order shown above.

Factory, Mine, Dark Forest, Brickworks, Mushroom Island, Candyland, Airport, and In My House have closed rail loops. Their cars use the same Matter collision and derailment rules. Crossing lights warn before the train arrives, and the roads offer detours.

Mushroom Island and Castle both have two-leaf bridges that rise and lower on a timer.

For a quick check, use `/?race=2&map=brickworks&seed=3&feature=now&dino=now` in dev. Set `map` from the table to preview any world. `feature=now` brings the first shortcut warning forward to one second of driving. The route closes for six seconds after the warning if the crossing is clear. Each world has an open alternate route. The shortcuts and movers pause during Stop & Answer breaks.

## Race music (2026-11-22)

All 16 worlds have distinct themes. Beach & Harbor has a syncopated pluck and triangle-bass track, **Buoy to Buoy**. Airport, Brickworks, Candyland, Castle, In My House, Mushroom Island, Frozen Tundra, Volcano, Mine, and Underwater now have 96–128-beat arrangements with contrasting middle sections. City uses short pulse replies between lead phrases. Dino keeps its opening drive and leaves more space in later phrases. The game uses Dino for every world boss and restores the world track when the boss is defeated or leaves.

Every world plays at full configured music gain through the dry signal path while driving, including turbo and the final stretch. Question breaks and pauses fade over 300 ms to 70% gain through a 3 kHz low-pass filter.

The renderer targets −17.5 dBFS RMS with a −1.5 dBFS sample-peak ceiling before Opus encoding. Live uses the same per-track gain from `src/audio/musicGains.json`. Pre-render applies the same loudness rule to its runtime buffers.

FFmpeg `volumedetect` measured the shipped Opus files. Race music, including the Dino boss track, spans **−18.6 to −17.5 dBFS mean (1.1 dB)**. The Results duration includes its ring-out.

## Question types: Math, Reading, JBQ (2026-11-22)

- **Join screen:** each player panel has a **QUESTIONS** row with three chips, **Math**, **Reading**, and **JBQ**. Any combination can be on, but at least one stays on. Left/right moves the focus ring and A toggles the focused chip. A on any other row still means Ready. Choices save to the racer profile right away.
- **Math** is addition on Easy, Easy+, and Normal, and multiplication on Hard. **Reading** is the picture/word spelling questions (place-word questions are Reading only). Older saves carry forward: Addition or Multiplication becomes Math, and Spelling becomes Reading.
- Car and buddy previews use the space freed by hidden JBQ rows. JBQ Basics gets an intermediate size, and JBQ with points and scope uses compact previews.
- **JBQ (Junior Bible Quiz):** with JBQ on, two more rows appear: points (**Basics / 10 / 20 / 30 pts**) and scope (**Week / Month / All**). The scope row shows the current question numbers, e.g. "Week 145–168".
  - **Basics** = the fixed set Q1–144 (10-point questions; `JbqLevel` 'basics', `Challenge.jbq.points` stays 10). The scope row is hidden (Join and Admin), the pool never grows beyond 144, wrong answers still come from the 10-point level.
  - **Player limits** (`jbqAllowed` in `src/challenge/jbqScope.ts`, `raceQuestionTypes` in `src/challenge/jbqRules.ts`): Basics, 10 and 20 pts work with 1–2 players, 30 pts with 1 player only. At race start JBQ is turned off for that player for that race only (Math + Reading if nothing else is on); the saved profile is untouched. On the Join screen the JBQ chip is crossed out and the QUESTIONS caption reads "JBQ: 1 player only" or "JBQ: 1–2 players" while the joined count disallows it; it updates as players join and leave.
  - Point levels: 10s = Q1–288, 20s = Q289–480, 30s = Q481–576. Data: `src/data/jbq.json`, built by `npm run jbq:data` from the cleaned CSV `data/jbq/Junior_Bible_Quiz_2016_MASTER_fixed.csv` (text fixes are listed in `data/jbq/FIXES.md`).
  - Schedule: `src/data/jbqSchedule.ts` (Nov 2026 – Mar 2027). **Week** = the latest schedule row on or before today (review and meet weeks give the review range). **Month** = the next REVIEW or MEET row on or after today. **All** = the whole level. After the schedule ends, Week and Month mean All.
  - **Pool growth:** each racer's correct answers per question are saved in localStorage `brainracer.jbq.v1`. When every question in the pool has been answered correctly `CHALLENGE.jbqMasteredCorrect` (2) times, the previous week's chunk is added, and so on. Not-yet-learned questions come up `jbqUnmasteredWeight` (4×) as often. Quick races without a profile keep progress in memory only.
  - **Missed questions:** a missed JBQ question returns after that player sees two other questions, even across driving breaks. The answer choices are shuffled, and each miss schedules another review until the player answers correctly.
  - **Answers:** 4 options. The wrong ones come from the whole point level, the same question type first, preferring similar length (`jbqLengthRatio`). Quote questions show 4 verse answers: the reference in bold blue, then as much verse as fits, ending in "…".
  - **Panel:** question card on top, four full-width answer rows (slot order top to bottom, with the button badge on the left). Fonts fit the card: 40–76 px in 1–2 player views, down to 26 px in 4-player quadrants for the longest verses (`HUD.jbq`). Dev preview: `?dev=JbqPanelTest` (`V` view count, `N` sample, `C` correct, `X` wrong).
  - Test another date with `&jbqDate=2026-12-10` (also changes the Join screen ranges).
- **Wrong answer reveal (all question types):** after the short wrong shake, the correct answer turns green and grows to fill the answer area for `CHALLENGE.wrongRevealSec` (2 s), then the next question comes.
- **While Driving is retired:** the Answer mode row is gone and a saved "drive" style loads as Stop & Answer. The drive code still compiles but is unused.

## Up to four players (2026-11-21)

Plan and decisions: [docs/FOUR_PLAYERS.md](FOUR_PLAYERS.md).

- **Menus:** Title ("Press (A) to play") → **Join** (`SetupScene`, four panels; any pad or keyboard layout claims the next panel with confirm, picks a unique racer, car and buddy, then confirms **Ready**. A Ready panel is locked until B un-readies it. Only P1 can press Next, once everyone joined is Ready) → **Track select** (`TrackSelectScene`, P1 picks the world and starts) → race. 1–2 players use the same screens.

- **Quick test:** `?race=3` / `?race=4` (P1/P2 keyboard, P3/P4 gamepads 0/1, idle if missing).
- **Profiling:** Admin Settings → **Perf overlay** shows FPS, 1% low, frame ms and the costliest sections (`cars`, `physics`, `render`, `sys:<name>`) from `src/core/perfProbe.ts`. Off = one boolean check per hook. Admin → **Performance test** runs an auto-driven 30 s race with 2 or 4 players and freezes the numbers on screen (`src/scenes/dev/FourViewPerf.ts`).

## Racer presets, question panel, boost FX (2026-11-20)

- **Setup** has one **Racer** row per player instead of Difficulty plus question toggles; the racers are the **shared profiles** (see Shared profiles below). Each profile has its own level, question types and race time (a new profile gets its level and race time from its **age band**: 0–5 Easy ★, 6–7 Easy+ ★½, 8–9 Medium ★★ (internal id `normal`), 10+ Hard ★★★; 10+ also gets the 3 min race, the others 5 min. `src/core/ageBands.ts`; the profile still stores `difficulty` + `roundSec`, the band is derived from difficulty. JBQ runs on its own level, unaffected. The Join card shows "Age 8–9 · Medium ★★"). A player defaults to the profile their slot used last if it still exists, else the n-th profile, else **New Profile** ("Make your racer!"); two players can't pick the same racer. **Race length** = `?round=` if given, else Admin Mode's 60 s, else the longer of the racers' times (`teamRoundSec`). The pick is `PlayerSettings.profile` (a shared profile id); `applyRacerProfile` (`src/core/racerProfiles.ts`) copies the settings into `difficulty`/`questionTypes` for ChallengeSystem. New-profile defaults: `NEW_RACER` / `NEW_RACER_DEFAULTS` in gameConfig.
- **Admin Settings → Racer Profiles** lists every profile, 2 per page with a Page row at the top. Each block: **Age** (cycles the 4 bands and sets level AND race time), Addition, Multiplication, Spelling, Race time (still adjustable after Age; one question type always stays on), **Rename** (on-screen LetterPicker; duplicate names rejected case-insensitively) and **Delete** (confirm, defaults to No; removes the shared profile for every app, its saved unlocks, and any player slot pointing at it). Rename and delete go through `shared/profiles.ts`; the game settings are in `Settings.racerProfiles`, keyed by profile id. **Racer Unlocks** cycles all racers. Reset to defaults leaves profiles and their settings alone. Paging math: `src/ui/menu/paging.ts`.
- **Question panel:** the question area is tinted and separated from the answers by a divider bar with a "PICK ONE" pill. **Xbox** face buttons show colors only (no letters); Switch keeps its letters.
- **Scoreboard** shows a flag and **n/3** world-completion goals for this race (green at 3/3, stays there).
- **Picture signs on place buildings are gone** (WorldBuilder `addSign` removed); the guide buddy and goal ring mark destinations.
- **Per-racer unlocks** (`src/core/racerProgress.ts`, storage `brainracer.racers.v1`): each profile has its own worlds, cars/buddies (earned order) and admin overrides, keyed by shared profile id. A new profile has no entry: City plus the starter cars (Dino, red kart) and buddies (`STARTER_CARS`, `STARTER_GUIDES`) only; nothing leaks from other profiles or Admin overrides (unit-tested). Setup shows each player only their racer's cars/buddies; the Map row lists any world **either** racer has (finishing it unlocks it for both).
- **World rewards:** every world has its own cars and one buddy (`WORLD_REWARDS` in `src/config/collectibles.ts`; a test enforces full coverage). Three goals give every racer in the race the next world plus one item from the world just played, different between the two players when possible; once a racer owns all of a world's items they get none there. The world's buddy comes after at most 2 cars in a row (`BUDDY_EVERY_REWARDS`). Results reveals the world once, then a named reel per racer ("<NAME> GOT …").
- **Admin Settings → Racer Unlocks** (was Content Locks): pick a racer, then Worlds pages or a page per world (cars + buddy), each Auto / Open / Locked for that racer only.
- **Boost FX:** during turbo the guide buddy goes calm (`GUIDE.boostTrailEverySec`, `boostSparkles`, `boostTwinkle`), and turbo flames/lines/aura are tunable (`REWARDS.turboFlamesPerSec` 50, `turboLinesPerSec` 24, `turboAuraScale` 10). A rough 2x-throttle check still showed a large fps drop during turbo in both old and new builds, so the main boost cost is probably elsewhere (boost jump, camera, flavor reward); not yet profiled.

## New Profile in Setup
- The Racer row cycles every racer (`racerIds`) then **New Profile** (hidden at `NEW_RACER.maxCustomRacers`). Setup keeps this as a local `isNew` flag per column; nothing special is saved. New Profile shows "Make a new racer!" and only starter cars/guides. Both players may pick it; real racers stay unique.
- START RACE with New Profile opens `NewRacerPanel` (`src/ui/menu/NewRacerPanel.ts`, logic in `newRacerLogic.ts`) for P1 then P2, driven only by that player's input: name (LetterPicker; duplicate names refused; Back on empty cancels to Setup) -> "How old are you?" with four big choices (0–5 · Easy ★ / 6–7 · Easy+ ★½ / 8–9 · Medium ★★ / 10+ · Hard ★★★), which sets level and race time and creates the racer (no separate Level step). Question types and JBQ choices are no longer asked here: the new profile takes what that player had on the Join screen (or the defaults). Back steps back.
- **Flow (newprofile-flow):** while a player's Racer row is on New Profile their Join card shows only that row, "Make your racer!" and "= make racer" (questions, JBQ, car, buddy are hidden and unreachable). Confirm opens `NewRacerPanel`; Back returns to New Profile with nothing created. On finish the new racer is selected, the panel is **not ready**, and the cursor sits on the Questions row so the player picks questions / JBQ / car / buddy and confirms to be Ready. (Before, `updateNewPanel` readied the panel, so a solo player hit NEXT at once.) `SetupScene.inputBlockedUntil` swallows input for `NEXT_GRACE_MS` after the panel closes so the finishing press cannot ready or advance. Phone: after creation tap NEXT.
- On GO: `createRacer`, settings saved immediately, player switched to the new id, then the next new player or the race starts. Test: `tests/e2e/menus.spec.ts`.

## Map progression and bosses

- The campaign starts in City and advances through Factory, Beach, Mine, Farm, Sky City, Dark Forest, and Underwater before reaching Brickworks. Complete three shared destination objectives in one race to unlock the next world. Results shows the new world's poster first, then a slowing reel lands on the earned collectible. Confirm advances to an available successor or plays the current world again. **Setup** returns to world and loadout selection.
- Each player chooses a car and companion from their racer's own collection (see "Per-racer unlocks" above). All racers can equip the two starter cars and two starter companions. A three-objective race can award one of the world's cars or its companion, from a catalog of 75 unlockable vehicles and 16 companions. Existing Comet unlocks and equipped selections carry over to Bomb Buddy.
- Open hidden **Admin Settings** with F9 or hold LT+RT and press Start. **Racer Unlocks** sets each world, vehicle, and companion to Auto, Open, or Locked per racer. Auto follows earned progress. **Admin Mode** opens the full catalog for play, defaults rounds to 60 seconds, and grants no completion unlocks. Switching it off restores normal access and the racers' own race times. Explicit time overrides still apply.
- **Bosses by map** are Dino in City, Rock Monster in Mine, Octopus in Beach & Harbor, Robot in Factory, Giant Chicken on Farm, Thundercloud in Sky-city, Walking Tree in Dark Forest, and Landshark Underwater. All use the same twin-beam, ram, and boarding rules. After three qualifying hits, press the right face button nearby to control the boss for 20 seconds of driving. A police swarm drives toward the ridden boss. Remaining cruisers drive away when the ride ends. Use `&dino=now&laser=now` to check any boss.
- The striped Harbor lighthouse stands beside the northeast waterfront road. The tower stays upright per camera and its stronger beam sweeps from the lantern. Factory headlights are aligned to the chassis, and the player cruiser siren plays at half its previous gain with the same bright tone.
- The Mine track “Crystal Switchback” has a fuller and faster 132 BPM arrangement, with its new Opus file bundled in the game.

## How to play / test
- Full game: `play.bat`, or `npm run dev` → http://localhost:5173/racer.html (the root is the game launcher; press A / Space three times to start).

Invalid values fall back to the defaults; saved settings never override them. `play.bat --round 240 --every 12 --music 0.5 --sfx 0.7` (alongside `--questions-off`) turns the flags into these params. Logic + tests: `src/config/launchOptions.ts`.

For a manual build and preview, set the variable before `npm run build`. The launch presets `?questions=words|math|mixed` apply spelling, addition, or both to everyone for that race. Question settings are handled in `src/config/questionsPolicy.ts`.

- Test hooks: `&dino=now` (dino appears next to players in 2 s), `&laser=now` (dino fires lasers soon and often), `&train=near` (Industrial: a wagon passes P1's nearest rail point), `&dinohits=N` / `&dinoride=N` (dino hits to defeat / ride seconds; console `__dinoDefeat()`), `&heat=100` (police chase P1), `&rewards=1` (keys 6/7/8/9 = rainbow/shockwave/ghost/magnet).

## How the game plays now
- **Menus:** The bottom face button joins (up to 2 players) and confirms. In Setup, use left/right to change options and Confirm on any option to start. Moving up from the first row wraps to the last row.
  Setup includes per-player racer, question types (**Math**, **Reading**, **JBQ**, see above), car, and companion. The map is shared. Questions are always **Stop & Answer**. Round length, question interval, and volumes also support launch options.
- **Multiplication** uses factors from 1 through 3 on Easy, 5 on Easy+, 9 on Normal, and 12 on Hard (level 4). Any combination of enabled types works in both answer modes. Older Words, Math, and Mix settings carry forward as Spelling, Addition, and both, respectively.
- **Camera:** facing-up. Each player's view rotates so their car points up (car at 52% height). 2 players = always split screen.
- **Question breaks (default "Stop & Answer"):** shared team timer. Every ~10 s of driving the world freezes for everyone, but the round clock keeps counting down. The four answer positions match the face-button diamond. Xbox shows Y/X/B/A (top/left/right/bottom), and Switch Pro shows X/Y/A/B for the same positions. Answer with face buttons, D-pad buttons, WASD/arrows, 1–4 (P1) or `, . / M` (P2). Wrong → sad sound + new question. Right → ✓ (2P waits for teammate). All right during the race → "GO!" → turbo + boost jump for everyone.
- **"While Driving" style (retired, hidden):** three answers (left, top, and right face buttons) in a panel centered below the car, a timer bar, and a slowdown after a miss. In both styles, driving freezes at zero and open questions remain answerable. Results includes those answers.
- **Right answer reward:** turbo (1.6× speed, flames), a **boost jump** over traffic/buildings (lands on drivable ground), **punch-through** (traffic/props thrown aside, speed kept), plus a random bonus (rainbow/shockwave/ghost/magnet).
- **Guide buddies:** a pink flying dino (dino-car) or a twinkling star (kart) leads directly toward the destination, including when it is off-screen. It circles the goal when near or waiting. Each player sees only their own buddy. There is no on-screen destination text: players follow their buddy, and the goal ring marks the place. The team score and timer sit at the top left in single player and at the top center in split screen.
- **Goals:** shared place destinations (bakery, school, …). Both players must arrive (20 s teammate wait). Jumping through counts. +3 points per arriving car.
- **Dinosaur:** when one player reaches the goal early (2P), the dino drops in and slowly circles the goal until the teammate arrives, sometimes sweeping laser eyes. Three qualifying hits defeat it, with a team hit counting twice when both players ram it within 1.5 s. Each player gets 2 points. A beaten boss turns into a big pulsing crystal in the boss's color for 8 s (`src/entities/BossCrystal.ts`, art `src/art/crystal.ts`). **Press the right face button once within 435 px of it** to hop on, ahead of other vehicles. Either player can board: the first to finish hopping on **claims** it (no banner; `dino:claimed` gives a `DINO_RIDE.claimPoints` bonus) and each rider gets their own boss mount (separate `Dino` per rider in `DinoSystem`, own 20 s timer). The crystal poofs when the window ends or everyone has boarded. Mounts walk over buildings, toss traffic and push each other apart. While riding, the vehicle-switch button fires that mount's laser eyes (`DINO_RIDE.laser*`: short charge, sweep straight ahead, cooldown); it zaps traffic only and never touches either car. Each rider returns to a car when their own ride ends; the timer pauses during that player's question. The ride timer pauses during questions. In solo it appears at half time at the current goal.
- **World:** seeded 48×48 city, beveled 3D buildings, picture signs on place buildings, ramps, ~25 light traffic cars/trucks, police with a gentle siren (bump only, despawn off-screen).
- **Traffic:** civilian drivers brake for cruisers, and player cruisers flash red and blue. Every 1.5 s, cleanup can recycle up to three off-screen cars. Cars qualify after five seconds in a queue or five continuous seconds yielding to police outside every player's view. Replacements favor clear lanes within the existing traffic limits. Night-map car halos follow the chassis.
- **Words:** `manifest.csv` labels → `src/data/wordAliases.json` (single simple words) → `src/data/wordMeta.json` (category, `simple`, `confusableWith`). Multi-word labels are Hard only. After edits: `npm run assets`.
- **Audio:** all synthesized (Tone.js). Music = JSON note tracks in `src/audio/music/tracks/` (optional `.mid` overrides in `src/audio/music/midi/`).

## Admin settings

Hold both triggers and press **Start** on a gamepad, or press **F9** on a keyboard. The game keeps these shortcuts hidden. Use left/right to adjust an option and Confirm from that row to accept and close. Advanced pauses every other running scene and resumes them on close. In a race the Start press also opens the pause menu underneath. Source: `src/scenes/ConfigScene.ts`.

- **Music / Sound FX volume**, **Round length**, **Time between questions**: saved in the normal settings (URL params `?round ?every ?music ?sfx` only override when actually present). Round/interval apply to the next race.
- **Audio mode** Live / Pre-render / Files (`audioMode`, default Files since 2026-11-20: it fixed the ONN box's audio clock from ~0.6x to ~1.0x). **Files** plays pre-rendered Ogg Opus files shipped in `public/audio/` (rendered once on the dev PC by `npm run audio:render`), so a weak box never synthesises or renders anything: at unlock it fetches `audio/manifest.json`, decodes the 35 sfx/loop files and the engine loop (about 0.3 s on a desktop), then decodes the 19 music tracks one after another (title first). Decoding uses a 32 kHz (music) / 44.1 kHz (sfx) `OfflineAudioContext` and each buffer is trimmed/padded to the manifest's exact sample length, so Opus padding never moves the loop points; they then go through the same buffer playback paths as Pre-render (engine Sample uses the file's loop too). Until a track is decoded its music stays silent (never live-synthesised); sfx fall back to live synthesis until their file is in. If the manifest cannot be fetched the sfx are rendered offline instead; a missing music/engine file is rendered offline too. Debug readout line 3 shows `mode files  sfx n/N  music k/M`. Pre-render details (same playback code): Pre-render renders sounds once into AudioBuffers (Tone.Offline) and plays them with plain native nodes, so the race audio graph is a few AudioBufferSourceNodes instead of live synths (for boxes whose audio render thread cannot keep up; symptom: debug `clock` well below 1.00x). Applies live: switching on starts the background render (sfx first, ~35 buffers in a few seconds on desktop; then the 19 music tracks, title first; the buffers are not persisted, every page load re-renders). Until a sound or track is ready the live version plays, and a running track swaps to its buffer at the same phase with a 0.3 s crossfade. Switching to Live stops using buffers (they stay cached). Details: sfx = mono 44.1 kHz buffers (rate 1, pan 0, trimmed) played through StereoPanner and Gain with the same TRIM/voice limits; `siren` (both loudness variants) and `skid` are seamless loop buffers; music = stereo 32 kHz, one loop (ring-out folded back onto the start) per track, rendered in 4 s windows because one big offline render is several times slower. The decoded PCM buffers total about 223 MiB once everything is cached. A `rate` option on sfx now also changes the length (it is a playbackRate), where live mode only scales pitch. Code: `src/audio/bufferUtils.ts` (pure helpers, tested), `sfx.ts`, `musicPlayer.ts`, `engineSound.ts`.
- **Re-rendering the files**: `npm run audio:render` (needs ffmpeg on PATH and Edge; takes about two minutes on the development PC). It starts vite on port 5186, runs the dev scene `src/scenes/dev/AudioRender.ts` in a muted headless Edge (same offline code as Pre-render, incl. MIDI overrides), and encodes Opus (music 96 kbps stereo, sfx/loops 64 kbps mono) into `public/audio/{sfx,music}/` plus `manifest.json` (exact sample length and rate of each source PCM, loop flag, BPM/beats/loop seconds, decoded music levels, `sourceHash`). Short loops (siren x2, skid, engine) are encoded as 3 back-to-back copies and the middle one is used. `sourceHash` covers the renderer, synth sources, loudness normalization and `src/audio/music/**` (CRLF-normalised); `scripts/audioManifest.test.ts` fails with 'audio sources changed: run npm run audio:render' when they differ. Commit the regenerated assets and live gains. The shipped audio totals about 14.4 MiB.
- **Admin Settings → Audio** groups Music, Sound FX, engine and playback settings. **Engine level** sets the engine-only gain to 25%, 50%, 75% (default), or 100%. It saves per device and applies live. A shared filter reduces low-frequency rumble in all engine modes.
- **Engine sound** Full / Lite (one saw + lowpass) / Sample / Off. Live. Sample = a 2 s idle-pitch loop (saw + square -> lowpass, no vibrato) rendered once and played as a looping buffer; playbackRate follows speed (55..170 Hz ratio), gain follows the Full curve, boost is +5% rate and +0.04 gain. Independent of Audio mode.
- **Audio buffer** Fast / Balanced / Safe = Web Audio `latencyHint` interactive / balanced / playback. Applies after reload.
- **Schedule ahead** 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3 s Tone look-ahead. Live. Larger = more starvation tolerance, more latency.
- **Compressor** On/Off: master -> compressor -> limiter -> out, or master -> out. Live.
- **Frame-rate cap** 60 / 30 (Phaser `fps.limit`). Applies after reload.
- **Debug readout** (bottom-left, always on top): `FPS`, audio `state`, `clock` (audio-clock advance / wall-clock over the last second; ~1.00 healthy, lower = audio starving), `stalls` (1 s windows with clock < 0.5 while visible), `lat` base/output latency ms, `ahead` look-ahead s, `sfx` live sfx voices, `eng` engine mode. A third line adds `mode live|pre`, `sfxReady n/N` (pre-rendered sfx buffers incl. 3 loops), `music cached k/M` and `RENDERING` while offline jobs are queued or running.
- **Traffic** Normal / Reduced (~50%) / Minimal (~25%) (`traffic`, applies to the next race). `scaledCount(n, level)` in `deviceTuning.ts` (rounds up, never below 1 for a positive count) scales every ambient-vehicle limit in `TrafficSystem.init` (city/harbor/underground cars, industrial regulars + freight trucks, farm pickups/tractors/combines), read once at race start. Police, dino, parked and story vehicles are untouched. Spawns stay seeded; there are just fewer.
- **P1 gas / P2 gas** A button / Trigger / Auto (`gas: [p1, p2]`, gamepads only, keyboards unchanged). A = max(RT, A) as before; Trigger = only RT gives throttle (A still confirms/answers); Auto = full throttle unless LT > 0.1 (so braking/reversing still works). Why: on the ONN in TV Bro, holding a button on the second controller flips TV Bro into controller-as-mouse mode; analog triggers avoid it. Applied only in `InputManager.get(player)` (races), so `any()`, menus and the LT+RT+Start combo always see the raw pad. Logic: `gasThrottle` in `src/input/mapping.ts`.
- **Input readout** (with Debug readout on): a top-right block (clear of the bottom timer bar and the centered scoreboard) lists each connected pad as `p0 Xbox b[0,7] L.00 R.85 x.12 y-.50` (pressed button indices, LT/RT values, left stick) and `keys <last 3 key codes> + age`. It reads `navigator.getGamepads()` and `window` keydown directly (a deliberate exception to rule 8, diagnostics only).
- Audio also tries `resume()` on `statechange`, tab-visible, and any key/pointer press if the context is not running.

## Shared profiles (2026-11-22)
